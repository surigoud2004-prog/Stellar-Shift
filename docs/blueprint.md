# **App Name**: Stellar Shift

## Core Features:

- Hexagonal Game Board Rendering: Visualize a game board using hexagonal tiles, populated with celestial entities, abstracting an 8x8 conceptual grid into a visually engaging layout.
- Celestial Entity Population: Randomly populate the hexagonal grid with 6 distinct types of 'Celestial' entities at the start of a new game or after matches.
- Swap Mechanics & Match Detection: Enable players to swap adjacent celestial entities; validate swaps to ensure they result in a match of three or more identical entities.
- Gravity System Simulation: Implement a gravity-like system where celestial entities fall into empty hexagonal spaces created by successful matches, and new entities populate from the 'top' (or outer layer) of the grid.
- Supernova & Black Hole Effects: Trigger unique visual and logical effects for special matches: a 'Supernova' that clears a full row or equivalent segment for matching 4 entities, and a 'Black Hole' that removes all entities of a matched color for 5 entities.
- Goal Tracking & Visual Progress: Track player progress towards primary goals, such as 'powering a spaceship' or 'unlocking ancient runes', displaying completion status and next objectives.
- Dynamic Lore Generation: Utilize an AI tool to generate short, thematic lore snippets, ancient rune descriptions, or spaceship status reports, dynamically presented upon achieving specific game milestones or triggering unique combo-chains.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant yet deep violet-purple, suggestive of cosmic energy (#BB70FF), providing a sense of mystique and power.
- Background color: A very dark purplish-gray (#271F2E), creating the expanse of deep space and enhancing the primary color's vibrancy.
- Accent color: An electric blue (#0D68F2), analogous to the primary but offering strong contrast and a sense of futuristic technology or energy bursts.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), for a technical, modern, and cosmic feel suitable for titles and key game elements.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), offering a neutral, clean, and highly legible appearance for game information, scores, and narrative snippets.
- Celestial entities will be represented by 2D polished icons of glowing planets, crescent moons, star-shaped crystals, and swirling nebulas, crafted in a clean, vibrant, and high-contrast vector art style on a transparent background. Each icon will have distinct shapes and glow effects, aligning with the 'Celestial Gems' theme.
- Emphasize a clean, focused game board within the viewport, with side panels or overlays for scores, objectives, and dynamic lore, ensuring an immersive experience.
- Implement a sleek glass-morphism style for UI frames, especially for score tracking and level progress displays, to provide a modern, transparent, and immersive overlay effect.
- Subtle yet impactful animations for entity swaps, matches, falling entities, and especially dramatic effects for 'Supernova' and 'Black Hole' events to enhance visual feedback and immersion.
